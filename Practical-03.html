Why I Chose to Volunteer?

I took the decision to take charge as the voluteer as I saw many poor families around my locality. It hurt me too much when I got to know that some families can't even afford one time meal for themselves. Also I heard somewhere that any small kind help to the needy can make a big difference in the society.

How You Can Help?

  • Donate even small amount of food to the needy ones.
  • Gather your family, friends and neighbours to spread this idea.
  • Participate in seminars and other awareness programmes.
